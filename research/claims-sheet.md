## Problem framing (their vocabulary)

Class8 describes a market where "per mile rates in a lane can fluctuate by a full dollar in a single day," worked by dispatchers tracking "market prices, fuel costs, driver preferences, ETAs, Hours-of-service, pickup and drop-off schedules." On the board itself, "loadboard postings are often stale and have inaccurate or missing details," yet "you can only call about one load at a time," and "most calls result in you waiting on hold or finding out the load is gone." A posting's headline rate is therefore not a profit number: it ignores the deadhead miles to pickup, the fuel burned across loaded plus empty miles, and the fields that are simply absent. Nothing on the board surface ranks postings by true profit per total mile, or flags the ones too incomplete to trust, before the first call is placed. A pre-call true-profit ranking layer fills that gap. It complements rather than competes with the existing products: Voice AI gets a prioritized call list instead of a raw board, and Dispatch AI gets cleaner inputs for its multi-leg planning.

## Red flags: what a pitch must not contradict

- **No driver surveillance, ever.** The Drive page mocks competitors' "Driver facing spy "coaching" cameras" and "AI-powered eye tracking to detect if you're blinking too slowly" as features Class8 refuses to build. Anything surveillance-flavoured is anti-brand.
- **ELD/HOS is regulated and self-certified.** Never propose editing, auto-fixing, or working around logs; their positioning is compliance without cost, and the ELD "is 100% free and will be forever."
- **Carrier data ownership is the founding story.** PR Newswire: data "that has historically served only outside interests"; EnerTech: "carrier-authorized OEM vehicle data" and a model that "keeps carriers in control of how their data is used." Never assume scraping broker/OEM data or sharing carrier data with third parties.
- **Do not pitch a replacement or another disconnected tool.** CEO, Business Wire: "most don't need more software; they need fewer barriers." Voice AI, Dispatch AI and Load Optimization are the core bet; a build must sit alongside them, and Class8 is an OEM-native operating layer distributed through Daimler, Volvo and Mack, not a load board or a DAT competitor.
- **Do not claim unhappy users.** No complaint corpus exists in their words or anywhere checked; pitch only pains Class8 itself names (stale postings, one-at-a-time calling, empty miles, dispatcher overload).
